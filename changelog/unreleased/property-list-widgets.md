### New

- **admin:** `DitoListField` and `DitoObjectField` edit lists and objects of the `properties` of `useDitoForm()`, laid out in their default slot with the slot props `{ item, index }`, in which widgets edit the properties of each item. Widgets without `name` edit the items of lists of values, and items of several `forms` are laid out by their `type`. The lists create, delete and reorder items like inlined lists, new items get the defaults of their properties, items show that they contain errors, and errors are revealed in collapsed items.
