### New

- **ui:** The placeholder `%popup` styles the panel of popups and menus, as used by `DitoMenuButton`, `DitoDatePicker` and the color and multiselect types, and `%popup--animated` adds the enter animation of larger popups. Menus and select lists open at once, calendars and color pickers animate. See [Placeholders](docs/api/ui.md#placeholders).
